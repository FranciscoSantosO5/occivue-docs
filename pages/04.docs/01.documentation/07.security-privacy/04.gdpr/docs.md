# GDPR and Lawful Camera Use

OCCIVUE operates in a European context where GDPR and other privacy rules can apply to camera deployments.

Using OCCIVUE does **not automatically make every camera use GDPR-compliant**. Customers remain responsible for the appropriate legal basis, notices, access controls and operational procedures for their camera use. OCCIVUE's own controller/processor obligations depend on the contractual and data-processing arrangement.

For a deployment-specific privacy assessment, use the current contractual and policy documentation.
