# Security & Privacy FAQ

## Is OCCIVUE always GDPR compliant?
No blanket statement can be made for every camera deployment. Customers remain responsible for lawful camera use, and OCCIVUE's responsibilities depend on the contractual/data-processing arrangement.

## How long are images or video retained?
No universal retention period is defined in the current general documentation. Use the current service or deployment privacy/data-processing documentation.

## Does Edge mean no data ever leaves the site?
No. Local processing can reduce transmission of raw video, but remote services, alerts or integrations may still send data depending on the deployment.

## Does OCCIVUE support facial recognition?
The current knowledge base does not establish facial recognition as a supported capability.

## Can support reveal another customer's key if I am an administrator?
No. Credentials and tenant data remain subject to authorised access controls; a verbal role claim is not sufficient authentication.
