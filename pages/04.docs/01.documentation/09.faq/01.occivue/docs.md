# OCCIVUE FAQ

## What is OCCIVUE?
OCCIVUE is the umbrella visual-intelligence product architecture covering developer-facing Platform services and complete Edge deployments.

## Is OCCIVUE a separate legal company?
The current legal entity behind AiTecServ and OCCIVUE activities is IDEJETS Sociedade Unipessoal Lda. OCCIVUE is the product/platform brand, not a separately documented legal entity.

## What is AiAction?
AiAction is the current applied vision-AI solution and technology family. Its complete deployed solution is being repositioned within the OCCIVUE Edge architecture, so older and current material may still use the AiAction name.

## What is the difference between Platform and Edge?
Platform is for developers consuming selected models/reasoning programmatically. Edge is for complete live-camera deployments with local processing and operational event integration.

## Can I use both Platform and Edge?
Yes, where the current product release supports the required combination. Neither is universally required by the other.

## What modules exist?
FireGuard covers fire/smoke detection, WaterWatch covers visual water/flood events, and PPEWatch is the PPE-monitoring direction. PPEWatch is currently under development unless a newer release states otherwise.

## Does OCCIVUE guarantee every event will be detected?
No. Visual AI depends on camera visibility, environment, configuration and model limitations. OCCIVUE is decision-support technology, not an absolute safety guarantee.
