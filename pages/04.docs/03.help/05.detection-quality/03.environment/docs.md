# Scene and Environmental Factors

Visual-AI performance can change with:

- camera angle and field of view;
- distance and apparent target size;
- resolution and compression;
- darkness or backlighting;
- fog, haze or rain;
- reflections and glare;
- occlusion and moving objects;
- lens contamination;
- scene density and visual clutter.

Validate the selected module against the real environment rather than relying on a universal accuracy or distance claim.
