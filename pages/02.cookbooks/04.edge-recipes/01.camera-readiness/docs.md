# Assess an Existing Camera for Reuse

1. Confirm that the camera is powered and reachable from the intended Edge network.
2. Confirm authorised credentials and stream access.
3. Identify the usable stream mechanism, such as RTSP.
4. Record codec, resolution and frame-rate options.
5. Validate field of view against the target event.
6. Check lighting, occlusion and scene quality.
7. Validate the stream with the selected AI capability.
8. Record whether ONVIF or vendor-specific integration is required.

Passing the network/stream checks does not guarantee model performance; the real scene must be validated for the selected capability.
