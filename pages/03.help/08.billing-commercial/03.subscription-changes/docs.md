# Subscription Changes and Cancellation

Platform upgrade, downgrade, suspension and cancellation behaviour is release-specific and coordinated with the implemented billing/API-management architecture.

Edge software/support terms are governed by the customer's contract or quotation.

Do not infer refund rules, cancellation rights or data-deletion timing from generic SaaS conventions. Use the current commercial terms for the applicable account.
