# Suspected Leaked Credential

1. revoke or rotate the credential through the authorised account mechanism as soon as possible;
2. replace the credential in authorised applications and secret stores;
3. review usage through authorised account tooling if available;
4. investigate repositories, logs or communications where the credential may have been exposed;
5. contact support if you cannot perform the authorised rotation/revocation process.

Do not paste the leaked credential into the support chatbot.
