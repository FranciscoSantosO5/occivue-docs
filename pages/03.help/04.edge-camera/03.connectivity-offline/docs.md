# Connectivity and Offline Behaviour

OCCIVUE Edge processes video close to the cameras, reducing dependence on continuous cloud inference.

If the local camera stream and processing services remain available, local inference may continue through an external-network interruption. However, remote notifications, monitoring, synchronisation, updates and external integrations may be delayed or unavailable.

Exact offline behaviour is deployment-specific.
