# FireGuard

FireGuard is the OCCIVUE/AiAction visual-intelligence capability focused on early detection of smoke and fire-related visual events.

## How it works

FireGuard uses **optical camera imagery** and AI-based visual detection. Thermal cameras are not a fundamental requirement for the demonstrated smoke-detection approach. Model output can be combined with IDMR so persistence, probability, apparent area and monitored-zone importance can influence event escalation.

## Long-range test evidence

Under test conditions, FireGuard has identified smoke visible at approximately **30 km** using optical imagery. In one referenced configuration, a **2 MP optical camera without zoom** was used and the visible smoke column occupied less than approximately **3 mm of image height** on the displayed image.

These are contextual test results, **not guaranteed detection specifications**. Practical range depends on optics, resolution, atmosphere, weather, terrain, smoke size/contrast, compression and camera position.

## Deployment factors

A high, unobstructed viewpoint is generally more useful for long-range smoke awareness than a cluttered or obstructed view. Existing compatible IP cameras may be reusable when they provide the required stream access and image quality.

## Limitations

Darkness, fog, heavy rain, haze, low contrast, obstructions and poor image quality can reduce visibility. FireGuard can produce false positives and false negatives and does not replace firefighters, civil-protection authorities, emergency procedures or legally required systems.

Exact event geolocation and wildfire spread prediction are not general current core capabilities.
