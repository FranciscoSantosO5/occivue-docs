# Camera Connectivity

The newer OCCIVUE Edge/AiAction direction is camera-agnostic and can use common IP-video mechanisms such as **RTSP**. **ONVIF** or vendor-specific interfaces such as **VAPIX** may be used when appropriate for discovery, control or integration.

Camera-agnostic does **not** mean universally compatible. A camera must provide a usable stream and satisfy codec, network, authentication, image-quality and module-specific requirements.

Compatibility should be validated before production deployment.
