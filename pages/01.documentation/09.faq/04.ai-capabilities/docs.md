# AI Capabilities FAQ

## Does FireGuard require thermal cameras?
No. The demonstrated smoke-detection approach uses optical imagery and does not fundamentally require thermal cameras.

## Can FireGuard detect smoke at 30 km?
Tests have included smoke visible at approximately 30 km under specific conditions. This is test evidence, not a guaranteed detection range.

## Does WaterWatch measure water depth?
Not by default. WaterWatch is visual detection, not automatically a hydrological depth sensor or hydraulic model.

## Is PPEWatch available in production?
PPEWatch is currently under active development unless a later release explicitly changes that status.

## Does PPEWatch identify workers by name?
No supported current capability establishes facial or employee-identity recognition. PPE detection is not biometric identification.

## What is IDMR?
IDMR is contextual decision/reasoning logic around model outputs. It can consider factors such as probability, apparent detection area, persistence and monitored-area category before escalating an event.

## Does IDMR eliminate false alarms?
No. It is designed to reduce operational noise but does not guarantee zero false positives or false negatives.
